Maximum Rope Area

How much area can n unit-length soft ropes enclose?

You are given n pieces of perfectly flexible, inextensible soft rope, each of length 1. You may arrange the ropes in any way you like to enclose the largest possible total area.

Rules

  • Each rope has length 1 and cannot be stretched.
  • Ropes are perfectly flexible and can be bent into any shape or tied together end-to-end.
  • Ropes may not cross or overlap.
  • The goal is to maximize the total enclosed area using one or multiple closed loops.
