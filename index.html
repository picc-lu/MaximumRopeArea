Maximum Rope Area

EN 中

How much area can n unit-length soft ropes enclose?

Rules

  • Each rope has length 1 and cannot be stretched.
  • Rope ends cannot be left dangling; every end must be connected to another rope end, attach to the same rope, or rest on another rope.
  • Ropes may touch or overlap one another, but they cannot cross through each other (no transverse intersection).
  • The maximum area of any single enclosed region cannot exceed 1.
  • The goal is to maximize the total enclosed area.
✔️ Allowed: Touch / Overlap
✔️ Allowed: End rests on rope
❌ Not Allowed: Cross through
❌ Not Allowed: Dangling end

How to Contribute

We welcome contributions of new configurations or improved exact bounds for any value of n!

Option 1: Submit a Pull Request (Recommended)

  1. Fork this repository.
  2. Add your configuration to the ropeData array in js/script.js.
  3. If you have an image (e.g., 10.png), upload it to the data/ folder.
  4. Commit your changes and submit a Pull Request.

Option 2: Send an Email
